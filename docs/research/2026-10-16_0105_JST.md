Two independent fresh runs per method completed with the frozen500-noise plus
maximum50-STE setup. Trained and quantized parameters, native PNGs, common
predictions, input identities, all retained checkpoint hash/RNG records, and
estimated bits matched exactly within each method. This is a minimal check on
one calibration input per method, not multi-seed performance evidence.

Final retained artifacts occupied at most11.32 MiB per encoded image. A single
rolling checkpoint occupied at most15.62 MiB. Atomic replacement temporarily
requires two checkpoints; the storage gate includes that headroom. Successful
jobs retained final trained/quantized parameters and verified their saved PNGs
before removing their rolling checkpoint. Failed jobs would retain their state.

The measured cumulative cost of four encodes and their four shared evaluations
was0.265 GPU hours. Adjusting historical full20 costs by the fresh/prior timing
ratio on the identical input, while retaining the planned50% workload allowance,
forecasts38.81 GPU hours for the maximum authorized scope, below the40-hour cap.
This is a forecast; the hard guards remain active. Single-image rate is never
used as a substitute for the fixed20 mean.

The user authorized the full experiment and private result storage, then allowed
Issue49 to run concurrently with older result transfers. All-transfer completion
is no longer a launch prerequisite. Only individually verified archived old
intermediate checkpoints may be deleted locally after matching their saved
SHA1; first/latest states per datum and small input/code/model/result dependencies
remain. Every deletion is audited. The running transfer is not modified.

The initial eight calibration conditions now use the same fixed20 images and
rate-only lambda selection rule. No calibration conditions are frozen yet, and
no heldout measurements or performance improvement are claimed. The two phases
will remain separate in the final numerical tables and plots.
